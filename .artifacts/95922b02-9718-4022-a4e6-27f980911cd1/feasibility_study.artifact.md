# AquaWatch: Feasibility Study

A brief assessment of the project's viability across technical, operational, and economic dimensions.

## 1. Technical Feasibility (High)
*   **Tech Stack**: Flutter and Firebase are industry-standard for scalable cross-platform apps.
*   **AI Integration**: Smartphone camera technology is sufficiently advanced to support basic colorimetric and turbidity analysis for parameter estimation.
*   **Offline Support**: Existing Flutter plugins (e.g., `sqflite`, `connectivity_plus`) make background synchronization reliable.

## 2. Operational Feasibility (Medium-High)
*   **User Adoption**: High mobile penetration makes citizen reporting accessible.
*   **Authority Involvement**: The project provides a "verification-ready" platform that reduces administrative overhead for government bodies, incentivizing their participation.

## 3. Economic Feasibility (High)
*   **Development Cost**: Low initial cost due to open-source tools (Flutter) and Firebase's pay-as-you-go/free tier.
*   **Value**: Replaces/augments expensive physical monitoring stations ($10k-$50k each) with a virtually free, crowd-sourced alternative.

## 4. Legal & Privacy Feasibility (Medium)
*   **Data Security**: Firebase provides robust encryption and identity management.
*   **Privacy**: Requires clear user consent for GPS data collection, adhering to standard GDPR/local data protection guidelines.

## 5. Conclusion
The project is **technically viable** and **economically efficient**. The primary success factor will be ensuring consistent engagement from both local communities and government authorities.
