# AquaWatch: Project Gap Analysis

AquaWatch bridges critical gaps between official monitoring and community needs.

## 1. Spatial-Temporal Gap
*   **Gap**: Official stations are sparse and miss localized pollution events.
*   **Fix**: Real-time crowdsourcing for high-density, localized monitoring.

## 2. Trust & Validation Gap
*   **Gap**: Citizen data is often unverified and ignored by policy makers.
*   **Fix**: Authority-led verification workflow to turn community reports into trusted data.

## 3. Economic Gap
*   **Gap**: Professional testing equipment is expensive and complex.
*   **Fix**: AI image analysis estimating water quality from standard smartphone photos.

## 4. Accessibility Gap
*   **Gap**: Environmental data is often hidden in technical reports.
*   **Fix**: Interactive public mapping for instant visual health checks of local water.

## 5. Connectivity Gap
*   **Gap**: Data is lost in remote areas without internet.
*   **Fix**: Offline mode with background sync for continuous monitoring anywhere.
