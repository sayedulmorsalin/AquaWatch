# AquaWatch: Core Objectives

**Vision**: Democratizing water quality monitoring through community action and AI.

## 1. Empowerment
*   **Citizen Reporting**: Instant mobile reporting of water quality data.
*   **Data Democracy**: Giving communities tools to monitor their environment.

## 2. Intelligence
*   **AI Detection**: Automated analysis of water parameters via photos.
*   **Geospatial Insights**: Interactive mapping of water quality hotspots.

## 3. Trust
*   **Authority Verification**: Bridging community data with official validation.
*   **Real-time Alerts**: Notifying users of quality changes and emergencies.

## 4. Reliability
*   **Offline Support**: Seamless data collection in remote areas.
*   **Scalable Tech**: High-performance cross-platform experience (Flutter/Firebase).
