# AquaWatch: Project Scope

**Core Focus**: Mobile platform for crowdsourced water monitoring and authority verification.

## 1. Included (In-Scope)
*   **Citizen App**: Auth, photo submissions, location tracking, and hotspot map.
*   **Authority Dashboard**: Submission review, verification tools, and data analytics.
*   **Intelligence**: AI-based water parameter estimation from photos.
*   **Connectivity**: Offline data capture with background synchronization.

## 2. Excluded (Out-of-Scope)
*   **Hardware**: No custom sensor manufacturing or IoT development.
*   **Treatment**: No water purification or filtering control.
*   **Desktop**: No dedicated desktop software (Mobile/Web only).

## 3. Constraints
*   **Quality**: AI accuracy depends on camera and lighting.
*   **Internet**: Periodic access needed for sync and AI processing.
