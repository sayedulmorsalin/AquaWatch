# AquaWatch: Target Users

## 1. Contributors (Citizens)
*   **Locals & Volunteers**: Reporting water safety and pollution.
*   **Students & Educators**: Using the app for STEM learning.
*   **Recreational Users**: Anglers and swimmers checking water health.

## 2. Verifiers (Authorities)
*   **Government Bodies**: Verifying data and managing regional quality.
*   **NGOs**: Coordinating environmental audits and advocacy.

## 3. Analysts (Researchers)
*   **Academics**: Studying pollution patterns via crowdsourced data.
*   **Public Health**: Monitoring risks and contamination trends.

## 4. Summary
| Group | Goal |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Citizens** | Safety, Awareness, & Education |
| **Authorities** | Verification, Regulation, & Policy |
| **Researchers** | Data Analysis & Scientific Study |
