# AquaWatch: Target Users

The platform is designed to connect three distinct groups: contributors, verifiers, and data consumers.

## 1. Primary Users (Contributors)
*   **Concerned Citizens**: Residents who want to monitor the safety of their local water sources.
*   **Community Volunteers**: Members of watershed protection groups or local environmental NGOs.
*   **Students & Educators**: K-12 and university students using the app for STEM education and field research.
*   **Recreational Users**: Anglers, swimmers, and boaters who depend on clean water for leisure activities.

## 2. Institutional Users (Verifiers)
*   **Government Authorities**: Local environmental protection departments and water utility managers.
*   **Municipal Officers**: City planners and sanitation departments tracking urban pollution trends.
*   **NGO Leads**: Organization managers coordinating large-scale regional environmental audits.

## 3. Specialized Users (Analysts)
*   **Scientific Researchers**: Academics looking for high-density, crowdsourced data to study climate or pollution patterns.
*   **Environmental Activists**: Advocacy groups using verified data to influence policy and resource allocation.
*   **Public Health Officials**: Monitoring for early signs of water-borne health risks or contamination events.

## 4. Key Motivations
| User Group | Core Need | App Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Public** | Safety & Awareness | Submit reports & check map |
| **Authority** | Data & Regulation | Verify reports & manage alerts |
| **Researchers** | High-density Data | Analyze trends & export data |
