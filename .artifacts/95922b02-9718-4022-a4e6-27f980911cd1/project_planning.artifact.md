# AquaWatch: Project Planning & Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the phases of development to take AquaWatch from foundation to deployment.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Current Status: In Progress)
*   **Environment Setup**: Flutter & Firebase initialization.
*   **Authentication**: Secure login/registration for Citizens and Authorities.
*   **Data Architecture**: Designing Firestore schemas for Submissions, Users, and Hotspots.

## Phase 2: Core Reporting & Visualization
*   **Submission Workflow**: Camera integration and form for water quality reporting.
*   **Geospatial Tracking**: Automated GPS tagging for all reports.
*   **Map Interface**: Interactive dashboard to view global/local water health.

## Phase 3: AI & Advanced Synchronization
*   **AI Integration**: Implementing image analysis for parameter estimation (pH, TDS).
*   **Offline Mode**: Local caching of reports with automatic sync upon reconnection.
*   **Push Notifications**: Alerts for environmental emergencies and report status.

## Phase 4: Authority & Verification
*   **Authority Portal**: Dedicated dashboard for government/NGO verification.
*   **Validation Workflow**: Logic for approving community data and updating map status.
*   **Analytics**: Generation of trends and reports for policy makers.

## Phase 5: Testing & Deployment
*   **UAT**: Field testing with community groups and authorities.
*   **Performance Optimization**: App size reduction and map loading speed improvements.
*   **Launch**: Distribution to Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
